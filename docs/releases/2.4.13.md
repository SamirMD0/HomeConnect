# HomeConnect 2.4.13

A deposit handed over with money still owed now becomes one invoice for the full price, with the deposit's payments as its receipts, like the old system (sales invoice, then receipts against it). Admins can edit invoices and receipts directly. **Includes a PC database migration.** No phone app change, no relay change.

## Changed

- **Converting a deposit to a debt** (or marking it delivered with money still owed) creates one invoice for the **full price**. The payments already made on the deposit move onto that invoice as its receipts, with their original dates. Example: $1,000 item, $600 paid → invoice $1,000, paid $600, remaining $400.
- **The deposit keeps its history.** It still shows the full price, what was paid and its bill history. In the customer profile it is no longer listed beside the invoice, so the sale shows once.
- **Edit receipts:** every live receipt (on an invoice, a payment plan or in the Payments tab) has an **Edit** button for amount, date, method, reference and notes. It saves straight away.
- **No reason box:** editing an invoice, a receipt or a deposit no longer asks for a reason. There is no password prompt either. The audit history still records who changed what and when.
- **Undo a handover:** reverting moves the receipts back onto the deposit and cancels the invoice. It is refused once a new payment has been taken on the invoice.
- Converting to **installments** is unchanged: the plan covers only the unpaid part.

## Fixed

- The invoice details window showed the status as **Unknown**; it now shows the real status (for example **Partially paid**).

## Database migration

Runs automatically when 2.4.13 is installed. For each deposit already handed over with an invoice that is not cancelled:

- the invoice grows by what the deposit had collected, and loses the "Balance for" prefix in its description;
- the deposit's receipts move onto the invoice.

Balances do not change: each invoice's remaining amount, the customer statement and the dashboard's outstanding totals stay the same.

**Reports:** monthly "collected" and "new debt" figures now include these deposit receipts and full invoice amounts, as the old system counts them. Past months that had converted deposits may show higher figures than before.

## Operator checklist

1. Install desktop **2.4.13**.
2. Open a customer whose deposit was already handed over. Their invoice should show the full price with the deposit payments listed as receipts, and the same balance as before.
3. To correct a receipt, open the invoice and click **Edit** on the receipt.

## Verification

- Checked in the app on a local copy of a recent shop backup: a $1,000 deposit with $600 paid converted to one $1,000 invoice with three $200 receipts and $400 left. A receipt was edited and saved with no reason, and the conversion was reverted back to a pending deposit holding $600.
- Migration checked on a sample converted deposit inside a rolled-back transaction: the $400 remainder invoice with $50 paid after handover became a $1,000 invoice with $650 paid and $350 left, the same balance as before.
- Database tests: the full-price invoice carries the receipts and the statement closes on the same balance; the profile lists the invoice only; invoice and receipt edits save without a reason; undo moves the receipts back; undo is refused after a later payment.
- 572 financial, dashboard, customer and report tests (database suites included) and 95 frontend tests pass; typechecks and the production build are clean.
