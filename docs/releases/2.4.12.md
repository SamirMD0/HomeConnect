# HomeConnect 2.4.12

Converting a deposit now leads straight to an invoice you can find and edit in the customer profile. No PC database migrations, no phone app change, no relay change.

## Fixed

- **The invoice made when a deposit was handed over could be hidden.** On customers with many deposits, the profile's invoice list could fill up with deposits and leave out the new invoice. Invoices are now listed first; deposits keep their own tab.
- **The profile did not refresh after a conversion.** Converting, delivering or reverting a deposit now refreshes the customer's profile, activity, invoices and payment plans.

## Changed

- **Deposits tab in the customer profile:** a **Convert to Debt or Installments** button on each open deposit. After converting, the new invoice (or payment plan) opens right away.
- **View invoice / View payment plan** buttons on converted deposits, in the profile and in the deposit details on the Deposits page.
- **Invoices tab:** an **Edit** button on each invoice that is not cancelled (admins only), so the balance, description or due date can be corrected without opening the invoice first.
- Deposit payments already received stay on the deposit. Only the unpaid balance becomes the invoice.

## Operator checklist

1. Install desktop **2.4.12**.
2. When goods paid by deposit are handed over, open the customer, go to **Deposits**, and click **Convert to Debt or Installments**.
3. Check the invoice that opens shows the unpaid balance only. Use **Edit** on the Invoices tab if the amount needs correcting.

## Verification

- Checked in the app on a local copy of a recent shop backup: the Convert button shows on the profile's Deposits tab, the dialog explains where the invoice goes, and **Edit** on the Invoices tab opens with the invoice's amount, description and due date filled in. No conversion was submitted on that copy.
- Database test: converting a $400 deposit with $150 paid creates one $250 invoice, shown in the profile even with a list limit of one; editing it to $225 updates the profile and the statement; the deposit still shows $150 paid; a second convert is refused.
- 58 prepaid and customer-summary database tests and 59 unit tests pass; typechecks and the production build are clean.
