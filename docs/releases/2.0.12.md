# HomeConnect 2.0.12

A new look across the app, prepaid conversion, statements of account, and smarter customer imports with merging.

## New look

- **Redesigned app shell, dashboard, login and setup.** Logo blue is now the brand colour. Green only means money in or done, amber attention, orange serious, and red overdue. Pages sit on a tinted background instead of plain white.
- **Other pages** pick up the new colours, fonts and components but keep their existing layouts.
- **Page search.** The top bar's search box finds pages by English or Arabic name; Ctrl+K opens it from anywhere. The disabled "coming soon" icons are gone.
- **Row menus.** Prepaid, Ledger, Receivables and Products share one larger, English-only 3-dots menu. It also opens by right-clicking the row.
- **Arabic labels removed** from those four tables and the Convert dialog. Names people typed are unchanged. The shared stock, active/archived and Branch labels are English wherever they appear.

## New features

- **Convert a prepaid to a debt or installments.** On a pending, part-paid prepaid, an admin can hand the item over and turn only the unpaid remainder into one debt or an installment plan. Revert Delivery undoes it until a payment is recorded against the new debt or plan.
- **Statement of account.** Customers have a Statement tab and suppliers a statement table on their profile, with opening balance, debit, credit, running balance and closing balance. Suppliers also get a printable statement.
- **Merge duplicate customers.** An admin can merge two records of the same customer, either adding both balances or keeping only the kept record's balance. This cannot be undone. A `!` mark and a Same name filter find the duplicates.
- **Change a customer's branch.** The customer moves with their history after a Confirm prompt.

## Customer import

- Re-imports compare each customer's file balance with what they owe. A lower balance records payments, oldest due first, and a higher balance adds a debt for the difference. A blank cell or unmapped column never counts as zero.
- Same-name customers match within their own branch instead of creating duplicates. Each row can have its own branch.
- New debts require a due date, replacing the silent default of today.

## Migration notes

Two database updates, applied automatically on upgrade. Neither changes any existing row:

- `20261102120000_allow_customer_merge` lets a merge repoint a sale and its counter payment together.
- `20261102130000_add_prepaid_remainder_plan` adds the link from a prepaid to the installment plan it became.

## Operator checklist

Beyond the default [upgrade smoke test](upgrade-smoke-test.md):

- Open the dashboard, login, and a list page, and check they render with the new look.
- Printed documents: print an invoice, a receipt and a customer statement, and check they are unchanged.
- Prepaid: convert a low-value part-paid item to installments, confirm the customer's statement shows the remainder, then Revert Delivery and confirm it reopens.
- Right-click a row on Prepaid, Ledger, Receivables and Products, and check the menu opens.
- Supplier profile: check the statement closing balance matches the supplier balance, and print it.
- Merge two test customers only. A merge cannot be undone.
