# HomeConnect 2.1.0

## New

- **ERP reports.**
  - Sales: Sales Summary (by day), Sales by Product and Sales by Customer.
  - Cash & profit: Cash Flow (money in and out by payment method, day by day) and Gross Profit.
  - Suppliers: Suppliers Paid and Suppliers Not Paid.
  - Inventory: Stock Valuation and Low Stock and Reorder.
  - Each report has a period picker, summary tiles, a full table and CSV/PDF export.
  - Gross Profit and Stock Valuation use each product's current cost price.
- **Customer import.**
  - Credit (minus) balances in the old-system file become opening deposits.
  - The main branch is never an import destination; its customers who still owe money are shown as warnings.
  - The wizard groups rows by colour, flags names one letter apart, and shows a per-branch reconciliation table before you confirm.
- **Dashboard privacy.** An eye button in the dashboard toolbar blurs money and counts until it is pressed again, with no password. The choice is remembered on each computer.
- **Customer branch.** The customer profile shows the branch the customer belongs to.

## Changes

- **New look.**
  - The app has a light-gray frosted-glass background with a floating sidebar.
  - Page content stays on white panels.
  - Printouts are unchanged.
- **Side menu.**
  - Items are grouped as Customers (Customers, Transactions, Receivables, Deposits), Suppliers (Suppliers, Bills & Payments), Inventory and After-Sales (Repairs & Warranty).
- **Customer profile.**
  - The account summary shows Balance due, Overdue, Deposits held and Last payment.
  - Tabs read Invoices, Payment plans and Deposits.
- **Supplier profile.**
  - Payables wording: Balance payable, Total billed, Payments made, Credits received.
  - "New bill" and "Record payment or credit" are at the top of the page.
- **Repairs & warranty.**
  - Each job shows a progress tracker and a warranty banner.
  - The status dialog uses plain date labels and suggests the next step.
  - The job list has quick status cards and shows warranty on each row.
- **Reports.** Reports are in English only. Arabic names print in a clearer, lighter font, and printed customer statements use larger text.

## Fixes

- Supplier changes, service job cancel/reopen and adding a product from a supplier bill no longer ask for an account password.
- Admins may leave the reason blank when editing, cancelling or voiding customer debts, plans, payments, supplier records and service jobs; the history records "No reason given".
- Dialogs, confirmations and the product/inventory side panels cover the whole window again.

## Migration notes

No database changes or manual migration steps.

## Operator checklist

- Open Reports and run Sales Summary, Cash Flow and Gross Profit for this month and a custom range; export one to CSV.
- Open a customer profile: check the branch badge, the account summary and the Invoices, Payment plans and Deposits tabs.
- Open a supplier profile and record a disposable payment from the header button; confirm no password is asked.
- Cancel and reopen a disposable service job without a reason.
- On the dashboard, press the eye button to hide and show the numbers.
- Open any dialog (for example Edit customer) and confirm it covers the whole window.
- Import a small customer file that includes a credit balance into a non-main branch, and check the reconciliation table on Confirm.
- Print a report and a customer statement and confirm Arabic text is clear.
