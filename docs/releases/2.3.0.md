# HomeConnect 2.3.0

This release goes with the **Home Connect phone app 2.1.0**, a new APK install. The phone finds the shop PC more reliably, and supplier bills now work for payments as well as purchases, with several pages per bill.

## New

- **Pairing code lists every PC address.** The QR code in **Scanner Hub → Mobile Scanner** now carries all of this PC's network addresses, not only the first. The phone tries them all at once and keeps the one that answers, so extra network cards (Hyper-V, WSL, VPN) no longer stop the first connection. Older phones still read the first address. The code is also drawn larger, with a wider white border, so it scans more easily.
- **Bills for payments.** A supplier bill can be linked to a **payment**, or to a **purchase that received no stock**. Before, it could only link to a purchase with a receiving. One bill can be linked to both a purchase and its payment.
  - **Ledger rows:** in a supplier's **Bills & payments** list, rows with bills show **Bill photos (N)**. Clicking it opens the bills.
  - **Linking:** the **Link** picker lists purchases and payments.
- **Several pages per bill.** A long bill is one bill with pages. The viewer has **Previous page** / **Next page**, and the list shows **N pages**.
- **Replace a page.** **Replace this page** takes a new photo for that page. The old photo is kept as history, not deleted.
- **Delete a bill or a page.** Deleting keeps a record of who deleted it, when and why, and the photo file stays on disk. Admins can delete any bill. Staff can delete only a bill they took themselves, while it is unlinked and less than 24 hours old.

## Migration notes

- `20261114090000_supplier_bill_pages_and_payments` runs on install. It is **additive only**: 8 new columns and 3 indexes on `supplier_receiving_receipts`. Nothing is removed or rewritten. Every existing bill photo becomes a one-page bill.
- **Nothing to run on the shop PC.**

## Phone app 2.1.0 (installed separately)

- **Connects on the first try:** it tries every PC address with short retries and finds the PC again when its address changes. **More → Connection details** lists recent attempts. The QR scanner has a torch and zoom.
- **Bills:** photos on supplier payments, several pages per bill, retake and delete, and linking to payments from the Bills inbox.
- **Customers tab:** **Receivables** with total owed, overdue and at-risk totals, standing filters, and a Home card.
- **New design:** a new look on every screen, a centre **Scan** button, a **To do** bell (bills to link, overdue customers, unsent photos), and forms that stay above the keyboard.
- Needs this PC update for the bill features. On an older PC the phone shows "Update HomeConnect on the shop PC to use all mobile features".

## Operator checklist

- **Re-pair once:** after the update, open **Scanner Hub → Mobile Scanner** and scan the new code with the phone, so the phone learns every address.
- **Bills on a payment:** on the phone, record a supplier payment with a bill photo of two pages. On the PC, the payment row shows **Bill photos (2)**, and the viewer pages through both.
- **Replace and delete:** replace one page on the PC. Then, signed in as staff, delete a fresh unlinked bill from the phone. Deleting a linked bill as staff must be refused.
- **Full phone checklist:** `docs/mobile/release-checklist.md`.
