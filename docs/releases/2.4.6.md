# HomeConnect 2.4.6

Phone actions reach the shop PC in seconds, and the phone can do much more away from the shop. No database migrations. The phone needs nothing new to benefit from the speed-up; the new phone screens arrive with phone APK 2.4.0.

## Faster

- **A payment from the phone took about 5 minutes to reach the PC.** The PC now keeps one light connection open to the cloud relay while the shop is open. When a phone sends something, the relay wakes the PC at once and the PC applies it within seconds, instead of waiting for its 15-minute cycle. The relay answers this wait from memory, so it does not use the Neon database allowance.
- Keeping that connection open also keeps the free Render relay awake during opening hours, so phones no longer wait about 30 seconds for it to start.
- A phone's "refresh" now asks the PC to send fresh data straight away (at most every 2 minutes).
- Outside opening hours the PC checks every 5 minutes, and backs off on its own if the relay or internet is down.

## New over the cloud (with phone APK 2.4.0)

The PC now accepts these phone actions through the relay. Each is checked and saved with the PC's normal rules:

- **Stock:** receive goods, add, return, remove, damage/loss, and stock counts. Count corrections, removals and damage/loss need an admin phone; the phone's PIN stands in for the account password, and each one is recorded in the admin audit as `paired-phone`.
- **Prepaid items.**
- **Supplier purchases**, including a purchase made from a bill, which also attaches the bill. If the bill cannot be attached, the purchase still stands and the bill stays in the inbox.
- **Bills:** link a bill to a purchase or payment, add or retake a page, and delete a page or a whole bill.
- Each supplier on the phone now carries its 30 most recent purchases and active payments, so a bill can be linked away from the shop.

Repeat protection: a phone action that arrives twice is applied once. Stock changes use the phone action's id; receiving, purchases and prepaid items use their existing unique keys. A retaken bill page sent twice only keeps an extra history copy.

## Not changed

- The shop PC stays the authority; nothing is decided on the phone or in the cloud.
- Sales, payments and figures work exactly as before.
- No database changes.

## Operator checklist

1. Install desktop **2.4.6** on the shop PC and leave Home Connect open.
2. On the phone, record a small test payment over mobile data (Wi-Fi off). It should show **Done** within about a minute.
3. If it stays on "Waiting for the shop PC", open **Maintenance → Open logs folder** on the PC and send `combined.log`.
